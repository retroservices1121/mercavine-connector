# Mercavine Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [EFFECTIVE DATE]

Mercavine ("Mercavine," "we," "us," or "our") runs a bot-first online marketplace at mercavine.com. Shoppers can search, buy, and track orders on our website or by chatting with an AI assistant, including through our Grok Bot shopping connector. Sellers can join, list products, and connect outside sales and fulfillment services to sell through Mercavine.

This policy explains, in plain English, what information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how we protect it, and the choices you have. Mercavine is based in North Carolina, USA.

If you have questions, contact us at **privacy@mercavine.com** *(placeholder — to be confirmed)*.

---

## 1. Quick summary

- We collect what we need to run the marketplace: your account details, orders, shipping information, and (for sellers) business, inventory, and fulfillment information.
- **We do not store your full card details.** Payments are handled by Stripe and, for some orders, Shop Pay.
- **We never ask for or store your Amazon password.** If a seller connects an Amazon seller account, we store only an encrypted authorization token that Amazon issues.
- **We do not sell your personal information.**
- We share information only with the companies that help us run the service (such as Amazon, Shopify, Stripe, and our hosting providers), with xAI/Grok Bot when you choose to connect your bot, and when the law requires it.
- You can ask to see, correct, or delete your information, and you can opt out of marketing.

This policy applies to:

## 3. Information we collect

### 3.1 Information from shoppers

- **Account and contact information:** your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), and login details.
- **Shipping information:** shipping and billing names and addresses, and delivery instructions.
- **Order information:** what you searched for, what you bought, prices, order numbers, order status, returns, and shipment tracking details.
- **Messages and support requests:** what you send us when you contact support, and the shopping requests you make through chat or a bot (for example, "find me running shoes under $100").

### 3.2 Information from sellers

- **Account and business information:** your name, business name, business address, email, phone number, tax and identity details needed for payouts and legal compliance, and store settings.
- **Product and listing information:** product titles, descriptions, images, prices, and stock levels.
- **Inventory, order, fulfillment, and tracking data:** inventory counts, orders placed with you through Mercavine, fulfillment requests, shipping carriers, and tracking numbers.
- **Amazon authorization tokens:** if you choose to connect your Amazon seller account (see Section 4), we receive and store an encrypted authorization (refresh) token from Amazon. **We never receive or store your Amazon password.**

### 3.3 Payment information

Payments are processed by **Stripe** (through Stripe Connect) and, for some orders, by **Shop Pay** (operated by Shopify). Your full card number and security code are entered with and kept by those providers, **not by Mercavine**. We may receive limited payment details from them, such as the payment status, the last four digits of a card, card type, and transaction IDs, so we can confirm orders, issue refunds, and handle disputes. Sellers' payout and bank details are collected and held by Stripe.

### 3.4 Bot and agent connection data

When you use Mercavine through an AI assistant or bot (such as Grok Bot), we receive information needed to complete your request, such as:

- an identifier for the connected bot or account,
- the search or shopping requests the bot sends us on your behalf,
- the products, orders, and tracking information we send back, and
- connection and permission settings (for example, when you linked or disconnected the bot).

### 3.5 Device, log, and usage data

When you use the Service, our systems automatically record technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages and features you use, referring links, dates and times of activity, and error logs. We use this to keep the Service working, secure, and improving.

### 3.6 Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your cart and preferences, protect against fraud, and understand how the Service is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings; if you block some cookies, parts of the Service may not work properly. See also Section 6.3 about link tracking for sponsored listings.

### 3.7 Information from other sources

We may receive information from partners you use with Mercavine, such as Amazon (for connected sellers), Shopify (for catalog listings and Shop Pay orders), Stripe (for payments), and shipping carriers (for tracking updates).

## 4. Amazon seller account connections

Sellers can choose to connect an Amazon seller account so that **Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF)** ships Mercavine orders from the seller's Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA) inventory.

- **How the connection works:** you sign in on Amazon's own page using Login with Amazon and authorize Mercavine through Amazon's Selling Partner API (SP-API) authorization process. You enter your Amazon credentials only with Amazon.
- **What we store:** an authorization (refresh) token issued by Amazon, stored **encrypted**. We use it only to perform the actions you authorized, such as checking inventory, creating fulfillment orders, and getting shipment tracking.
- **What we never store:** your Amazon password.
- **Customer information shared with Amazon:** to fulfill an order, we send Amazon the shopper's name, shipping address, and order details needed to ship it.
- **Amazon customer information we receive:** any personal information about customers that we get through Amazon's services is used only to fulfill orders and related legal or tax obligations, is retained only as long as needed for that purpose, and is deleted within 30 days after the order is fulfilled unless the law requires us to keep it longer.
- **Disconnecting:** you can disconnect your Amazon account at any time in your seller settings or by revoking Mercavine's access in Amazon Seller Central. When you disconnect, we delete the stored token.

## 5. How we use information

We use information to:

- **Run the marketplace:** create and manage accounts, show search results, process orders, arrange fulfillment and shipping, provide tracking, and handle returns and refunds.
- **Power bot and chat shopping:** answer shopping requests sent through a connected bot or chat, and return product, order, and tracking information to it.
- **Support sellers:** onboard sellers, publish listings, sync inventory and orders, send fulfillment requests (including to Amazon MCF), and arrange payouts through Stripe.
- **Process payments and fees:** work with Stripe and Shop Pay to complete payments, and calculate Mercavine's fees.
- **Communicate with you:** send order confirmations, shipping updates, account and security notices, and support replies. With your permission or where allowed by law, we may send marketing messages, which you can opt out of at any time.
- **Keep the Service safe:** detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents, and enforce our terms.
- **Improve the Service:** understand how features are used, fix problems, and build new features.
- **Meet legal obligations:** keep tax and transaction records, respond to lawful requests, and resolve disputes.

## 6. Payments, fees, and sponsored listings

### 6.1 Mercavine orders (Stripe)

Orders placed and paid through Mercavine's own checkout are processed by Stripe through Stripe Connect. Mercavine charges a **3% fee** on these orders. Stripe handles card details and seller payouts under its own privacy policy.

### 6.2 Shop Pay orders

Some products come from Shopify merchants and are purchased through **Shop Pay**, using Shopify's Shop tools and a single-use payment credential. In these orders, your payment is handled by Shopify/Shop Pay, and **Mercavine does not charge a fee**. We pass the order details needed to complete the purchase (such as the product, your shipping information, and contact details) to Shopify and the merchant. Your use of Shop Pay is also covered by Shopify's privacy policy.

### 6.3 Shopify catalog search and Sponsored listings

We may show products from Shopify's catalog in search results. Some of these are **promoted listings, which are clearly labeled "Sponsored."** Shopify may pay Mercavine a commission when you click or buy through these listings. To make that work, links to these products include tracking parameters (such as `shclid` and `shcgid`) that let Shopify know the visit or purchase came through Mercavine. Sponsored placement does not change how we handle your personal information under this policy.

We share information only as described here:

- **Business changes:** if Mercavine is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that deal, subject to this policy.
- **With your permission:** in other cases when you ask us to or agree to it.

## 9. How we store and protect information

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect your information, and we align our practices for Amazon data with Amazon's Data Protection Policy. These include:

- **Encryption in transit:** information sent to and from the Service is protected with TLS (HTTPS).
- **Encryption at rest:** sensitive data, including Amazon authorization tokens and personal information, is encrypted when stored.
- **Access control:** only staff and systems that need information to do their job can access it, using least-privilege permissions.
- **Multi-factor authentication (MFA):** required for staff access to systems that hold personal information.
- **Logging and monitoring:** we log access to sensitive systems and watch for unusual activity.
- **Incident response:** we maintain an incident response plan. If a security incident affects your personal information, we will investigate, take steps to contain it, and notify you, affected partners (such as Amazon, where its data is involved), and authorities as required by law and our agreements.
- **Limited retention:** we keep personal information only as long as needed (see Section 10).

No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your account password private and let us know right away if you think your account has been compromised.
