Mercavine lets a shopper's agent search home improvement, tools, and electronics accessories, then hand the human one approval link. The connector is free. Each Mercavine seller is charged on their own Stripe account, and Mercavine takes a 3% application fee from that charge (not from the shopper). Shop Pay orders are paid on the merchant's checkout and do not carry that fee. The bot never sees or completes payment.
